Doraemon

A Famous Blue, Earless Robotic Cat from the 22nd century

Doraemon is sent to the 20th century by Nobita's future great-great-grandson, Sewashi Nobi, to prevent Nobita from having a disastrous future and to steer him toward a better life. Doraemon lives with the Nobi family and uses a four-dimensional pocket on his stomach to produce various futuristic gadgets to help Nobita with his daily problems, which often leads to comedic misadventures and moral lessons.

Gadgets

Anywhere Door

The Anywhere Door (Dokodemo Door in Japanese) is one of the most popular and frequently used gadgets in the series. It basically resembles a pink door that acts as a convenient portal.

Gadgets

Time Machine

The Time Machine allows the user to travel to any point in the past or future. It serves as the entrance to the Time Tunnel and lets the user visit different times and places by setting the desired date and location.

Gadgets

Take-copter

Take-copter or Bamboo Copter ("The Hopter" in the US English dub and manga) is a gadget of 22nd century which is used by humans mostly.

Latest Movies

Nobita's Earth Symphony

The story follows Nobita as he practices the recorder for a school concert. He meets a mysterious girl named Mikka, who invites him and his friends to the "Farre" (Hall of Music) on a planet where music is the primary energy source.

The group must use "Musician's Licenses" to master instruments and save the hall from Noise, a malevolent entity intent on erasing music from the entire universe.

Doraemon: Nobita's Chronicle of the Moon Exploration

Follows Nobita and- his friends to the moon, where they use special badges to create a "Rabbit Kingdom". After meeting a mysterious transfer student named Luca, they get involved in an adventure to save the "Espals" (aliens with psychic powers) from the evil Emperor Diabolo.
